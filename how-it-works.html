How it works

Ordering takes four steps

No account and no online payment. You pick, personalise and send. Rashida does the rest.

  1. 1

    Pick your gift

    Browse the shop and add your favourites to the cart.

  2. 2

    Personalise it

    Add the name, message and delivery time you want.

  3. 3

    Send on WhatsApp

    Your order goes straight to Rashida, who confirms the price with you.

  4. 4

    Pick up or receive

    Collect at NIMA or UPSA, or ask for delivery within Accra.

Common questions

How do I pay?

Rashida confirms the total and the payment method with you on WhatsApp once she has your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes, within Accra. The delivery fee depends on your location and is confirmed on WhatsApp. You can also pick up at NIMA or UPSA.

Can I change the name or colours?

Yes. Balloons, boxes, ribbons and cards can carry any name or message. Add it when you order or in the notes field at checkout.

How early should I order?

As early as you can, especially for custom names, printed phone cases and cakes. For urgent orders, message us on WhatsApp first.

Can you make something that is not on the site?

Yes. Send a photo or describe what you want and Rashida will price it.
