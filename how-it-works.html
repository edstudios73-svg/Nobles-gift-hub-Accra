How it works

Ordering takes four steps

No account and no online payment. You pick, personalise and place your order. Rashida checks availability and updates you on the tracking page.

  1. 1

    Pick your gift

    Browse the shop and add your favourites to the cart.

  2. 2

    Personalise it

    Add the name, message and delivery time you want.

  3. 3

    Place your order

    It goes straight to Rashida, who checks availability and confirms the price.

  4. 4

    Track it

    Follow every step on the Track your order page, then collect at NIMA or UPSA, or get it delivered nationwide.

Common questions

How do I pay?

Rashida confirms the total and the payment method once she has checked your order. You can see the total on the Track your order page.

How do I know if my item is available?

After you order, open Track your order and enter your order number and phone number. Each item shows as available or not available, with a message from Rashida.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver nationwide. The delivery fee depends on your location and is confirmed on WhatsApp. You can also pick up at NIMA or UPSA.

Do I need to book?

Yes. Our services are strictly by appointment, so message us with the date you need and we will confirm availability.

Where can I see prices?

Starting prices are on the price list. Rashida confirms the final price for your exact choices on WhatsApp.

Can I change the name or colours?

Yes. Balloons, boxes, ribbons and cards can carry any name or message. Add it when you order or in the notes field at checkout.

How early should I order?

As early as you can, especially for custom names, printed phone cases and cakes. For urgent orders, message us on WhatsApp first.

Can you make something that is not on the site?

Yes. Send a photo or describe what you want and Rashida will price it.
